# On-Call Rotation

The on-call rotation runs weekly, Monday to Monday, and covers all
production services owned by the team. The current schedule is managed
in the on-call tool - check there, not the wiki, since the wiki version
goes stale.

Every new engineer shadows one full rotation before taking a primary
on-call shift, so they see what a normal week looks like alongside
someone experienced before being paged themselves.

If you're paged, acknowledge within 5 minutes. If the issue is a SEV1 or
SEV2, follow the incident response runbook rather than trying to fix it
solo and quietly.

You can swap on-call shifts with a teammate as long as it's reflected in
the on-call tool before the shift starts - informal swaps that aren't
recorded there cause pages to go to the wrong person.

Compensation for on-call weeks follows the standard company on-call pay
policy documented in the HR portal, not in this engineering wiki.
